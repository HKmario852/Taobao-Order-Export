# Privacy policy

Order Exporter for Taobao (淘宝订单导出) does not collect, store, sell or send any data.

- It runs only on Taobao's 已买到的宝贝 page (`buyertrade.taobao.com/trade/itemlist/*`).
- It reads the order data that page loads, only after you click **Export all orders**, and keeps it in the
  page's memory until you close or reload the tab.
- When you click **Download Excel** or **Download JSON**, it saves a file to your computer through your
  browser's normal download. That file stays with you.
- It makes no network requests of its own, has no analytics or tracking, and uses no remote code.
- It does not read or export addresses, phone numbers or recipient names.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/HKmario852/Taobao-Order-Export/issues.
